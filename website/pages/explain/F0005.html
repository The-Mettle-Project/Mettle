F0005The effect pass spent more than its budget

Reported by the compiler as an error or warning.

mettle explain F0005

--effect-budget=N makes the cost of inferring the program's effects a contract: the steps the pass spends scanning bodies and running its fixpoint may not exceed N. They did.

--report-effects prints what the pass settled, one line per function that performs or needs anything, and the totals: functions seen, functions with an effect, fixpoint rounds, steps.

Fix: declare fewer effects, cut the call graph the fixpoint walks, or raise the budget deliberately.
