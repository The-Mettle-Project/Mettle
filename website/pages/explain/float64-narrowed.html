float64-narrowedA float64 computation runs in float32 on Metal

Reported by --explain after a verdict.

mettle explain float64-narrowed

Apple GPUs have no double. Where every operand is a float32 value or a literal that float32 holds exactly, and the result is only rounded back to float32, the computation gives the same bits in float32, and that is what is emitted. Anything else in float64 is refused.
