C0003A numerics contract is unproven for a kernel

Reported by the compiler as an error or warning.

mettle explain C0003

A kernel that declares @numerics(C) is a member of C, and the build proves C by running its harnesses. A member that no harness launches, or a harness that makes no claim with numerics_same, leaves the contract unproven, and an unproven contract is not stated.

Fix: launch the member from a harness of C and compare its output with another member's.
