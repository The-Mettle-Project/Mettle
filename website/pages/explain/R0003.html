R0003A rule could not decide

Reported by the compiler as an error or warning.

mettle explain R0003

A @rule fn returned verdict_gap: at this site it could neither prove nor refute the property it checks, and it says so rather than guessing. The build goes on. This is a warning so the gap is loud.

A rule reports what it can prove and announces what it cannot, the same standard the borrow analyser is held to. Typical gaps are a call through a function pointer or an extern the rule has no model for.

Fix: give the rule what it needs to decide (a direct call, a named function), or accept the gap.
