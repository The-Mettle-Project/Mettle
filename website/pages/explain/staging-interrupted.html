staging-interruptedAn operation sits between the staged copies and their barrier

Reported by --explain after a verdict.

mettle explain staging-interrupted

Copies from global to workgroup memory can be issued asynchronously when nothing but register arithmetic runs between them and the barrier that publishes them. A load, a store or a call in that stretch could see workgroup memory before the copies land.

Fix: move it after the barrier.
