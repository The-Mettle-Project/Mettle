Invent a machine.

Describe an instruction set in a Mettle file. The compiler checks it, assembles your program for it, and runs it.

{{MACHINE_CODE}}{{MACHINE_OUT}}

Faster than llama.cpp, on the same card.

Systm's Potentia runs LLMs on Mettle. Its kernels are Mettle functions, and yours can be too. They compile straight to PTX or Metal with no nvcc, C++ or Python in the pipeline, and one line launches one. Its build fails unless the GPU matches an independent CPU reference, logit for logit.

{{GPU_CODE}}

Gemma 4 12B and 26B-A4B, QAT Q4_0, on one RTX 5060 Ti, against llama.cpp from LM Studio with every layer on the GPU and one engine in VRAM at a time. Energy is board joules per token. At 16 requests, throughput is 473 against 446 tokens a second.

Everything else stays close to C.

16 whole programs, including parsers, compressors, interpreters and solvers. clang 22.1.6 on Windows, on an i7-12700K, best of 15 pinned runs. Under -march=native, clang's physics_grid returns a different checksum. Run them with tools/benchmark/mc.ps1 -Suite 3.
