the desktop is painted by damage, not by frames.

  nothing redraws unless something changed. moving a window marks the rectangle
  it left and the one it arrived in, and only that union is repainted and sent
  to the card. an idle machine repaints the clock corner once a second, about
  two thousand pixels, and nothing else

  every drawing call is clipped to the damaged rectangle, so painting the whole
  desktop inside a small clip costs almost nothing: the fills clip to zero
  width and the glyphs return before they touch a pixel

  the timer runs at a thousand ticks a second, so the pointer and the keyboard
  are read within a millisecond of moving

  a task that is waiting for a key is marked waiting and the scheduler skips
  it, so the shell costs nothing while it sits at the prompt and the desktop
  gets the whole processor while you drag

  when the svga device is there the pointer is a hardware cursor, so moving the
  mouse redraws nothing at all
