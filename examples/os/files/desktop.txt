the desktop, from the bottom up:

  the kernel looks for a vmware svga device on the pci bus. when it is there,
  the kernel sets the mode itself, writes commands into the device fifo, and
  hands the pointer to the hardware cursor. when it is not, the framebuffer the
  bios handed over at boot is used instead and the pointer is drawn by hand

  the wallpaper is painted once into its own buffer: a vertical gradient, two
  radial glows, a vignette, and a little ordered dither so the gradient does
  not band. every frame starts as a copy of it

  the dock is a strip of that wallpaper, box blurred, tinted, and rounded,
  baked once at startup. the icons and labels are drawn over it each frame

  windows carry a soft shadow built from rings of rounded outlines, a gradient
  title bar with an accent line under the focused one, and corners that are
  antialiased by sampling the circle nine times per pixel

  the pointer is a polygon, sampled nine times per pixel for the fill and again
  for a grown outline, so it has a dark edge and a soft one
