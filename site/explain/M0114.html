M0114Dereference of an unmapped constant address

Reported by the compiler as an error or warning.

mettle explain M0114

The pointer holds a small integer constant. The low 64K of the address space is never mapped on any supported platform, so this dereference faults.

Usually a placeholder that was never filled in, or an integer that was cast to a pointer by mistake.
